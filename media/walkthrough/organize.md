# Organize into folders

- **Edit Layout** (the pencil button) adds checkboxes. Uncheck a repository to hide it.
- **Drag** repositories onto folders, or drag folders into other folders.
- **New Smart Folder…** creates a folder that fills itself from a query, for example:
  - `is:conflict OR pipeline:failed`
  - `is:dirty AND age<7d`
  - `is:mr folder:Services`
- The layout is saved in your workspace (`.code-workspace` or `.vscode/settings.json`).
