# Save and share profiles

The **Profiles** panel starts with every visible repository on its current branch.

- Uncheck repositories, pick branches, and set where each one is cloned.
- **Save Profile** so you can switch back to it later with **CANOPY: Switch Profile…**.
- **Export File…** or **Copy Share Link** to send it to a teammate. Opening it shows exactly what will be cloned, switched or stashed, and nothing runs until they confirm.
